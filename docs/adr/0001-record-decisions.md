# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-10

## Context
Contributors and AI agents need to know *why* things are the way they are, or they re-open settled questions.

## Decision
Record each significant decision (architecture, dependency, process) as a short ADR in `docs/adr/`, copied
from `0000-template.md`. New dependencies need an ADR.

## Consequences
Decisions are reviewable in PRs. Superseded ADRs stay, marked "superseded by NNNN".
